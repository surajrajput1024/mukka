Privacy

Mohalla Mukka has no login, no ads and no account. Your best score, win streak, sound pack and language are saved only on your own phone (localStorage). Sound is generated on your device; no audio files are downloaded.

If the owner turns on the optional visitor counter (GoatCounter), it counts page visits without cookies and without personal data. If it is off, the game makes no network calls after loading.

Sharing a score creates a link that contains only the score. All characters are fictional and not based on any real person.
